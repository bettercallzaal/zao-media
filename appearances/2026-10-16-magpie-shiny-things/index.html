YouTube Magpie on X

What it covers

Magpie spent years in web3 gaming partnerships before going solo in February. His studio, Shiny Things, builds Fable Boy, a retro console that runs in any browser with 30 games, custom skins and leaderboards, and Waypoint, a space game where you own a whole solar system and AI crews fly your fleet. He demos both live, shows Doctrine, a card RTS, and explains why everything runs in the browser with no installs, free to play.

  • Try this: open Fable Boy in your browser and set a score on one of the 30 games.
