What it covers

The through-line: low-turnout governance is a security hole, and The ZAO is designed to close it.

  • The BonqDAO apathy attack - an attacker spent $4.4M buying the token and passed a proposal draining $20M from the treasury, on 2.9% turnout. No hack; the governance did exactly what it was built to do.
  • The ZAO's defense - the Respect token is illiquid (can't be bought or sold anywhere) and soulbound (once earned, it stays in your wallet). So $4.4M can't buy ZAO governance. Plus a seven-day yes/no vote and a veto-only period after.
  • ENS security council, Cardano and Solana governance updates, MetaDAO futarchy, the CLARITY Act.
  • AI-agent legal entities - Marshall Islands, Delaware, Bermuda, and the plan for a community-weighted AI agent (ZOL bot) that manages funds via a legal entity (MIDAO).
  • WaveWarZ - live-traded music battles that pay artists every trade. And ZAOstock, the Maine festival.
