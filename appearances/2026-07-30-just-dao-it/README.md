## What it covers

Adam and Zaal go from industry news into a deep dive on The ZAO. The through-line: **low-turnout governance is a security hole, and The ZAO is designed to close it.**

- **The BonqDAO apathy attack** - an attacker spent $4.4M buying the token and passed a proposal draining $20M from the treasury, on 2.9% turnout. No hack; the governance did exactly what it was built to do.
- **The ZAO's defense** - the Respect token is **illiquid** (can't be bought or sold anywhere) and **soulbound** (once earned, it stays in your wallet). So $4.4M can't buy ZAO governance. Plus a seven-day yes/no vote and a **veto-only period** after - "the same vote effectively stays open longer for nos only."
- **ENS security council**, Cardano/Solana governance updates, MetaDAO/futarchy, the CLARITY Act.
- **AI-agent legal entities** - Marshall Islands / Delaware / Bermuda, and the plan for a community-weighted AI agent (**ZOL bot**) that manages funds via a legal entity (MIDAO).
- **WaveWarZ** - live-traded music battles that pay artists every trade.
- **ZAOstock** - the Maine festival.
