What it covers

Danny is a full-stack blockchain developer from Nigeria and the builder of P2E Inferno, a live quest platform where communities set actions people can prove they did, and reward them. He lost his front-end job when COVID hit, played Axie Infinity to get by, learned to build through BuidlGuidl and Unlock Protocol bounties, and designed P2E Inferno from what he lived. Zaal and Danny walk through the quests live, talk about the verifier layer he wants to bring to WaveZStation, and then he turns the questions on Zaal about ZAOstock.
